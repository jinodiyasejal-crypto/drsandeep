Meet Dr. Sandeep Choudhary

Dr. Sandeep Choudhary is a dermatologist at Skinum Clinic, Indore. He diagnoses the cause of your skin or hair problem first, then plans a treatment that fits you.

The clinic offers both medical dermatology, such as acne, psoriasis and vitiligo, and aesthetic treatments, such as laser, peels and PRP. Every consultation is by appointment, so you get time with the doctor.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, consultations are by appointment so that the doctor can give you enough time.

How many sessions does laser hair reduction need?

It depends on the area and your hair type. The doctor will tell you the number of sessions after examining you.

Are the treatments painful?

Most procedures cause only mild discomfort. The doctor will explain what to expect before you begin.

How much do treatments cost?

The cost depends on the treatment and the number of sessions. Message us on WhatsApp for details.

How long until I see results?

It varies by condition. Acne and pigmentation usually need several weeks, and hair treatments take longer. Your doctor will give you a realistic timeline.
